Spaceback

Free up iPhone storage without deleting photos.

Every Live Photo is really two files: the picture, plus a 3-second video clip. That clip is often larger than the photo itself. Spaceback removes only the video and keeps the still — same resolution, same date, same location, same albums.

Nothing is uploaded. Spaceback has no account, no server, and no analytics. Your photos never leave your iPhone.

What else it cleans

  • Screenshots — swipe right to keep, left to remove
  • Similar shots — bursts grouped together, tap to pick what stays
  • Blurry photos — sorted worst-first
  • Large videos — listed by size

Nothing is deleted behind your back

Photos you swipe left go to a review list. You see them all together before anything happens, and you can pull any of them back out. Deletion happens once, at your confirmation, and everything lands in Recently Deleted for 30 days.

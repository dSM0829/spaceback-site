Spaceback

Privacy Policy

Effective 20 August 2026

Spaceback does not collect, store, or transmit your photos. All photo analysis happens on your device. There is no account, no login, and no Spaceback server.

1. Your photos

Spaceback needs access to your photo library to find what is taking up space. It reads photo metadata — file size, capture date, dimensions, and whether a photo is a Live Photo, screenshot, or video — entirely on your iPhone.

Photos are never uploaded, copied off the device, shared, or sent anywhere. Spaceback has no network capability for photo data.

2. What is stored on your device

To remember your choices between sessions, Spaceback saves a small amount of data inside its own app container:

  • Photo identifiers (local iOS references, meaningless outside your device)
  • File sizes and capture dates
  • Which photos you chose to keep, and which are waiting in the review list
  • Your daily sorting count and total space freed

This data stays on your iPhone. Deleting the app removes all of it.

3. Advertising

The free version shows ads through Google AdMob. Google is an independent third party and may collect the following to serve and measure ads:

DataPurpose
Device identifiersAd delivery, frequency capping, fraud prevention
Usage dataAd interaction measurement
DiagnosticsCrash and performance reporting
Coarse location (country/region)Regional ad compliance

Spaceback does not request App Tracking Transparency permission and does not track you across apps or websites. Ads are served in non-personalized mode. Your photos are never involved in advertising in any way.

Google's handling of this data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy. Purchasing Spaceback Pro removes all ads and the AdMob SDK stops requesting them.

4. Purchases

Spaceback Pro is a one-time purchase processed entirely by Apple. Spaceback never sees your name, payment method, or Apple Account. Purchase status is verified on your device using Apple's signed transaction records.

5. What we do not do

  • No analytics or behavioral tracking SDK
  • No account, email collection, or newsletter
  • No selling or sharing of personal data
  • No cross-app or cross-site tracking

6. Children

Spaceback is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

7. Your control

You can revoke photo access at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → Spaceback. Spaceback cannot function without it, but nothing is retained after revocation. Deleting the app erases all locally stored data immediately.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above is updated and the revised policy is posted on this page.

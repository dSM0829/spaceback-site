2. What the app does

Spaceback identifies photos and videos that take up storage on your iPhone and helps you remove them. Its main feature converts Live Photos into still photos by removing the attached video clip, which frees space without deleting any photo.

3. Deletion is permanent after 30 days

Photos removed through Spaceback go to Recently Deleted and are erased by iOS after 30 days, or immediately if you empty that album. Converting a Live Photo to a still permanently removes its motion once Recently Deleted is emptied. This cannot be undone.
